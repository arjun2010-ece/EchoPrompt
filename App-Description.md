EchoPrompt is a modern, privacy-focused teleprompter with voice and hands-free auto-scroll controls. Built exclusively for macOS and iOS, it runs 100% on-device via Safari and native WebKit.

✨ **Key Features**

* 🎙️ Voice-Controlled Scrolling — Navigate your script hands-free using voice commands powered by native speech recognition.
* ⏱️ Auto-Scroll Mode (No Voice Required) — Hands-free scrolling without voice input. Set your preferred reading pace (words per minute or scroll speed), and the teleprompter smoothly scrolls automatically.

* 🔒 **100% Private** — All processing happens locally on your Apple device using native browser APIs.
* 📱 **Works Offline** — Install to your Home Screen as a standalone PWA for offline access.
* 🌐 **Multi-Language Support** — Only support english at the moment.
* 🪞 **Mirror Mode** — Perfect for teleprompter glass setups (horizontal flip + 180° rotation).
* 📱 **Screen Rotation** — 90° clockwise rotation toggle built specifically for iOS devices in landscape mode.
* ⚙️ **Highly Customizable** — Adjust font size, spacing, colors, and themes.
* 📜 **Script History** — Automatically saves your recent scripts locally.
* 🎨 **Modern UI** — Beautiful dark/light themes with smooth animations.

🌐 **Platform & Browser Support**

* **Safari (macOS & iOS)** — Full support including PWA installation and native speech recognition.
* **Chrome for iOS** — Full functionality utilizing iOS WebKit engines.

📥 **Installing as a PWA on iOS/iPadOS**

1. Open the app in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"**.

🎯 **How to Use & Voice Commands**

* **Special Voice Commands:** Say *"go start"* (jump to beginning), *"go back"* (scroll back a few lines), *"go next"* (skip forward), or *"go finish"* (jump to the end).
* **Manual Navigation:** Tap any word to jump to that position, use the restart button ($\circlearrowleft$), or back button ($\leftarrow$).
